I was born in North Carolina, and graduated in 2017 from Appalachian State University with a B. S. in Applied Physics and various academic accolades. From there, I crossed a decent fraction of the world to obtain my Ph.D. in Astronomy from the Institute for Astronomy at the University of Hawai’i at Manoa. During my time in Hawai’i, I was on the practice squad for the UH women’s basketball team, met and married my wife, welcomed my first kid, and wrote a bunch of papers – a busy 5 years indeed! After my Ph.D., it was another haul back across the US to start my postdoc position at Ohio State’s Center for Cosmology and AstroParticle Physics ([CCAPP](http://ccapp.osu.edu)).

When not chasing exotic supernovae or building data reduction tools, I enjoy basketball, hiking, public outreach, and tiring out my young kids – some pictures below!
